Resume Levels

The right resume strategy for your career stage.

Every career stage requires a different approach. I tailor the depth, positioning, achievements, keywords, and overall strategy of your resume to match where you are now and where you want to go next.

Level 01

Entry-Level / Graduate

$100+

You may not have years of experience yet, but you still have value to communicate. Your education, internships, projects, skills, achievements, and potential are positioned into a focused resume that helps employers see what you can contribute.

Level 02

Mid-Level Professional

$200+

At this stage, simply listing responsibilities is no longer enough. Your resume should show progression, ownership, measurable results, and the skills that make you valuable in your field—not just describe what you were asked to do.

Level 03

Senior-Level Professional

$300+

Senior professionals need a resume that communicates influence and depth. I focus on high-value achievements, complex responsibilities, leadership, strategic contributions, and the evidence that separates you from other experienced candidates.

Level 04

Managerial / Director

$400+

Your resume should make your leadership impossible to overlook. The focus shifts toward teams, budgets, operational scale, business improvements, strategic decisions, stakeholder management, and measurable organizational impact.

Level 05

Executive / C-Suite

$600+

Executive resumes are not extended versions of ordinary resumes. They must communicate leadership vision, business impact, strategic authority, transformation, and the value you bring at the highest level—quickly and convincingly.

Level 06

Career Change

$300+

Changing careers requires more than changing a job title. I identify transferable strengths and reshape your experience so recruiters can understand why your background makes sense for the new direction you are targeting.

Pricing note: These are starting ranges, not rigid prices. Your final quote depends on career level, resume length, complexity, research required, turnaround time, and whether additional documents or LinkedIn optimization are included.
